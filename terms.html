2. The product and the service

FaultScope is a portable industrial fault-diagnosis terminal. It consists of a data box that reads live PLC state over Modbus TCP and a camera unit used to photograph the equipment or control cabinet. The FaultScope service analyses the photograph together with the live PLC state and returns a diagnosis: the likely cause of the fault, where to look, and a first safe step to verify it.

The first-year package (¥9,800) includes the terminal hardware, on-site installation and training, first-year software access, and remote support. Renewal (¥2,000 per year) covers software access, experience-library updates, and remote support.
