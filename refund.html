Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. 30-day refund window

You may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Contact us within the window and we will handle your request promptly.

2. How to request a refund

Email support@faultscope.cn (or call 17538802479) with your order number, the date of purchase, and the reason for the refund. We will confirm receipt within 2 business days.

Your subscription is fully refundable within the 30-day window. On refund, your subscription is cancelled and access to the diagnosis service ends at the end of the refunded period.

Refunds for online orders are returned through Paddle to the original payment method. Refunds for orders paid by bank transfer are returned to the original bank account. Once the refund is approved, it is issued within 10 business days.
