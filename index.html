Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use it?

A phone or computer to photograph and upload, and an internet connection. There is no hardware to buy and nothing to install — the analysis runs in the cloud.

What equipment data can I upload?

Fault codes, PLC readings, alarm logs, or a short description of the symptoms you observed. The more context you provide, the more specific the analysis report.

What if the analysis is wrong?

Every report includes verification checks — the measurements and observations that confirm or rule out the suggested finding. If the finding does not check out, follow the next verification step or contact support. If FaultScope does not work for your equipment, you are covered by our 30-day refund policy.

Does it need internet?

Yes. The analysis runs in the cloud, so you need an internet connection to upload photos and receive reports.

What happens at renewal?

Renew at ¥2,000 per year to keep receiving software updates, experience-library updates, and email support. You can cancel before the renewal date and will not be charged again.
