A stopped line costs money every minute

A junior technician stands at the cabinet not knowing where to start. A senior engineer gets the 2 a.m. call. FaultScope turns a fault into a guided check, so the person standing at the cabinet can find the cause with confidence — and the expert gets called less often.

1

Connect

The data box connects to the control cabinet and reads live PLC state over Modbus TCP. No changes to your program.

2

Photograph

Take a photo of the board or cabinet with the camera unit. The image and the live PLC state go together to the diagnosis service.

3

Diagnose

You get a grounded diagnosis: the likely cause, where to look, and the first safe step to verify it — plus the checks that would prove it wrong.

Frequently asked questions

Will it work with my PLC?

If your PLC exposes a Modbus TCP interface, FaultScope can read its state. Most modern PLCs support Modbus TCP, either natively or through a communication module. If you are unsure, send us your PLC model and we will confirm before you order.

What if the diagnosis is wrong?

Every diagnosis comes with verification steps — the measurements and checks that confirm or rule out the suggested cause. If the cause does not check out, follow the guided next step or contact support. If FaultScope does not work for your equipment, you are covered by our 30-day refund policy.

Does it need internet at the machine?

Yes. The terminal needs a network connection to reach the diagnosis service. The data box connects by Ethernet and the camera unit by Wi-Fi.

What happens after the first year?

Renew at ¥2,000 per year to keep receiving software updates, experience-library updates, and remote support. You can cancel before the renewal date and will not be charged again.
