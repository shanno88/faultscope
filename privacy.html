Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

FaultScope is operated by 洛阳市西工区道源网络销售商行 (sole proprietorship / 个体工商户), Luoyang, Henan, China. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: support@faultscope.cn or 17538802479.

2. What data we collect

  • Account and contact data: your name, email address, phone number, and company name, provided when you order or contact support.
  • Order data: what you purchased, when, and the delivery address. Payments are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), our merchant of record — we do not receive or store your card details.
  • Analysis data: the photographs and equipment data you upload for analysis (fault codes, readings, or observed symptoms).
  • Support communications: emails and call notes when you contact us.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to provide the FaultScope software (including returning analysis reports), to fulfil and support your order, to operate and secure the software, and to respond to your enquiries. We do not sell your personal data.

Fault cases are stored in your own account. We do not use your photographs or fault cases to improve the shared experience library unless you explicitly opt in.

4. Who we share data with

  • Paddle — processes your online payment as merchant of record. Paddle’s own privacy policy applies to the payment data it collects.
  • Service providers — hosting and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Legal obligations — we disclose data where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Retention

We keep account and order data for as long as your account is active and as long as required for tax and accounting records. Analysis data (photographs and equipment data you upload) is kept while your account is active, or until you ask us to delete it.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to export the data in your account. Contact support@faultscope.cn or 17538802479 and we will respond within 15 business days. Deleting your account also ends your subscription.

7. Security

We protect your data with access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

8. Cookies

This website is a static site and does not use tracking cookies.

9. Children

FaultScope is a business product and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in force is the one published on this page. Material changes will be notified to active subscribers by email before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@faultscope.cn or 17538802479.
